## 3. Guiding Principle: Your Financial Data Stays with You

We offer two products, and they handle data differently. No statement below applies to both unless it says so.

**Wealth Analyzer (the browser application).** Financial and household data you enter (income, expenses, assets, liabilities, goals, simulation outputs) is processed and stored **locally in your browser** (localStorage). We do not receive it, we do not store it, and we have no access to it.

This holds **except** where you yourself switch on an optional integration. Those features send data from your browser directly to a third party **you** choose, using an API credential **you** supply and store in your own browser:

- **AI document intake and AI portfolio suggestions**, if you enter your own AI provider key: the **content of the documents you upload** — bank and brokerage statements, tax forms, pension letters, images and scans — is transmitted to that provider, which is located in the United States.
- **Market-data, reference-data and news providers**, where you supply a key.
- **Custodian, CRM and order-management endpoints** that you configure yourself.

In each case you are the transmitting party, under your own contract with that provider. We are not a party to the call, we hold no credential, and we receive nothing. If you use the software with data subject to professional or banking secrecy, switching on such an integration is a disclosure to a third party and is your decision to assess.

**Wealth Analyzer Platform (the hosted service).** Data entered into the hosted service is stored **on servers operated by us and our sub-processors**. It does not remain in your browser. Where that data concerns the clients of a firm using the Platform, the firm is the controller and we act as its processor under a separate data processing agreement.

For that reason, the actual financial data you enter into the Software is, by design, outside the processing operations described below. If you clear your browser storage, that data is irretrievably lost from our perspective.

We therefore process personal data essentially only in connection with your purchase/subscription, our support and the operation of our website.

## 4. What Data We Process

### 4.1 On Purchase and Subscription

When you buy or subscribe to the Software, we process:

- **Contact data**: name, email address, and, where applicable, company name and billing address
- **Contract data**: plan chosen, contract start and end dates, invoice number
- **Payment data**: **We do not store credit-card data**, and no payments are taken through the website or the browser application today. When paid plans are introduced, the payment processor will be named here and in the table in section 6 before any charge is made.

**Legal basis**: performance of the contract (Art. 31 para. 2 lit. a revFADP; for EU users, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and statutory retention duties (in particular Art. 958f Swiss Code of Obligations, 10 years for accounting records).

### 4.2 On Support Requests

When you contact us by email or via a support form, we process:

- your email address and, where applicable, your name
- the content of your request, including attachments
- the metadata needed to respond

**Legal basis**: your request (overriding legitimate interest under Art. 31 revFADP; for EU users, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, if the request refers to an existing contract, contractual performance.

### 4.3 On Website Visits

When you visit our website, our hosting provider (or we) collect the following access data in **server logs**:

- IP address (truncated or anonymised where technically possible)
- date and time of access
- URL requested, response status, volume of data transferred
- referrer URL, user-agent (browser / operating system)

This data is used to ensure the operation of the site, to defend against attacks and for aggregated statistical analysis. It is normally deleted after 30 days.

### 4.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use **only technically necessary cookies** (e.g. session management and language selection). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. For the operation of the Software itself we use **localStorage** in your browser — where only your own inputs and preferences are stored.

Where we use additional cookies (e.g. for analytics) we obtain your prior consent (cookie banner). You may withdraw your consent at any time via the cookie settings.

### 4.5 Analytics (optional)

We use **no analytics or tracking tooling**. The application pages make no requests to any third party when they load, so no analytics provider receives anything. The only measurement that exists is the standard server log kept by our hosting provider (see section 6), which records the IP address and request metadata of each visit.

The Software performs simulations and calculations based on your inputs. Those calculations run locally on your device and constitute neither an automated decision nor a binding recommendation (see also section 8 of the [Terms of Service](./TERMS-EN.md)).
