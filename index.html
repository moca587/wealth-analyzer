How it works

From client meeting to portfolio in 90 seconds.

No build step. No backend. Open the file, fill the form, get a portfolio with citations.

Capture the client

Household profile, income, expenses, assets, liabilities, goals — plus address country so we know what wrapper applies. Two-client households fully supported.

2

The agent extracts asset-class consensus across all 16 institutional sources — overweight equities, underweight EM debt, gold tailwinds — and tilts allocation accordingly.

Regulatory wrapper

Built for cross-border wealth.

The platform encodes the regulatory wrapper rules so you don't have to remember them. When you set a Swiss client's address, the universe drops from 792 funds to 271 UCITS-passportable funds — automatically.

The AI agent literally cannot recommend US 40-Act funds to European retail clients. PRIIPs requires a KID. UCITS funds have one. US funds don't. Done.

  • US / Canada: US 40-Act ETFs and mutual funds (521 funds)
  • UK / EU / CH: Ireland + Luxembourg UCITS (253 funds)
  • HK / SG: UCITS + local listings (271 funds)
  • Asia / MENA / LatAm: UCITS via offshore wrappers
