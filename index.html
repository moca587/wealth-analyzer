Capabilities

The whole engagement, in one tab.

From the first data-capture field to the client-ready PDF — plan, analysis, proposal, and simulation, with tax and country rules built in. No platform fee, no consultants, no procurement cycle.

🧩

Financial Plan

Capture the whole household — two clients, children, income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and calendar-year goals — then let the plan match where your client actually lives.

  • Two-client households + children
  • ~500 account types across 19 countries
  • Goals as calendar-year targets
📊

Portfolio Analysis

Drop in a client's current holdings and see the book the way a risk desk does — concentration, diversification, and a blended risk score, with full metrics on every position.

  • Concentration (HHI) + diversification grade
  • Weighted yield & expense ratio
  • Per-position Sharpe / β / μ / σ / max DD
📜

Investment Proposal

Draft a proposed allocation and put it head-to-head with the current book. See expected return, volatility, and Sharpe move — net of the advisory fee — plus the goal-funding impact of switching.

  • Current vs. proposed, side by side
  • Advisory-fee & net-return modeling
  • Goal-funding impact of the switch
🎲

Monte Carlo simulation

1,000 log-normal paths (Box-Muller), real loan amortization, and configurable percentile bands. Goal funding as calendar-year probabilities, retirement drawdown, and sustainable spend solved to a 90% success target.

  • P10 / P50 / P90 wealth bands
  • Goal & retirement funding odds
  • Sustainable spend & achievable lifestyle
🏛

Tax-aware modeling

Federal + state/canton brackets for the US (12 states), Canada, UK, Australia, Germany, Switzerland (3 cantons), Japan, and Singapore — applied to income, and optionally to simulated returns.

  • Per-bracket marginal rates
  • Per-state / canton overrides
  • Optional after-tax simulation
📄

Client-ready reports

One click turns the whole analysis into a polished, multi-page PDF — cover, contents, plan, portfolio, proposal, and simulation — ready to hand to the client. Generated entirely offline.

  • Multi-page branded PDF
  • Plan · analysis · proposal · simulation
  • Fully offline, private by default
How it works

From first meeting to documented plan.

No build step. No backend. Open the file, fill the form, and get a stress-tested plan you can hand to the client.

1

Capture the plan

Enter the household, income, expenses, assets, liabilities, and goals — plus each client's country, so account types, tax, and inflation all self-adjust.

2

Analyze & propose

Load the current portfolio to see its risk and concentration, then draft a proposed allocation and compare the two — net of fees, goal by goal.

3

Simulate & report

Run 1,000 Monte Carlo paths for funding odds, retirement drawdown, and sustainable spend — then export the whole thing as a client-ready PDF.

Country-aware

Country-aware from the first field.

Set a client's country and the whole workbench adapts — the account types they can hold, the tax brackets that apply, the inflation region for the projection, and which fund wrappers are eligible. A Swiss client's universe narrows to UCITS-passportable funds automatically; a US client gets 40-Act.

No more remembering which wrapper applies where. PRIIPs requires a KID — UCITS funds have one, US funds don't — so European retail clients simply never see ineligible funds.

  • US / Canada: 40-Act funds · IRA / 401(k) / RRSP account types
  • UK / EU / CH: UCITS funds · ISA / PEA / pillar 3a wrappers
  • HK / SG: UCITS + local listings · CPF / MPF
  • Asia / MENA / LatAm: UCITS via offshore wrappers
