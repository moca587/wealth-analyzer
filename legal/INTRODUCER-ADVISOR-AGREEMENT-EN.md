## Recitals

**A.** PWI develops and commercialises "**Wealth Analyzer**," a browser-based Monte Carlo financial-planning software product (the "**Software**") and adjacent services.

**B.** The Advisor holds a professional network in the enterprise banking-software and wealth-management sector, including in particular relationships with senior representatives at Avaloq Group AG and other enterprise potential customers.

**C.** The Parties wish to formalise a commercial arrangement under which the Advisor introduces PWI to enterprise prospects and provides strategic advisory support on the resulting relationships, in exchange for a tiered revenue share and milestone bonuses on customers he sources — without granting equity or ownership rights in PWI.

**D.** This Agreement sets out the terms on which the Advisor is engaged as an independent contractor.

## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

**"Advisor Introduction"** means a written introduction (by email, in-person meeting with follow-up written confirmation, or comparable documented means) from the Advisor to a named individual at a Prospective Customer, with PWI cc'd or otherwise verifiably informed at the time of introduction.

**"Attribution Window"** means the period of **twelve (12) months** starting on the date of the Advisor Introduction, during which a Qualifying Contract must be signed for that Prospective Customer to become an Attributed Customer.

**"Attributed Customer"** means a Prospective Customer that (i) was the subject of a valid Advisor Introduction, and (ii) signed a Qualifying Contract with PWI within the Attribution Window. Once attributed, the Customer remains an Attributed Customer for the full Revenue-Share Period, regardless of later renewals, extensions or expansions.

**"Attributable Revenue"** means Net Revenue received by PWI from Attributed Customers under Qualifying Contracts and any renewals, expansions, upsells and add-on modules of those contracts, during the Revenue-Share Period.

**"Net Revenue"** means gross revenue actually collected by PWI from an Attributed Customer, less: (i) applicable VAT and other pass-through taxes, (ii) refunds, credit notes and chargebacks, (iii) direct third-party pass-through costs mandatorily deducted at the point of sale (e.g. payment-processing fees, marketplace commissions imposed by a distribution partner). For clarity, PWI's own operating costs, staff salaries, cost of goods, or overheads are **not** deducted.

**"Prospective Customer"** means an enterprise entity that PWI does not, at the date of the Advisor Introduction, already have (i) an existing commercial relationship with, (ii) an active sales opportunity with, or (iii) prior written contact traceable to a direct-sourced lead. Non-limiting examples of eligible categories include: Avaloq Group AG and affiliates; Temenos, Finnova, ERI-Bancaire and other core-banking software vendors; Swiss and international private banks; family offices; wealth-tech and RegTech firms.

**"Qualifying Contract"** means a legally binding contract signed with an Attributed Customer for the paid supply of the Software, related services, subscriptions, integrations, or licences, with a first-year committed value of at least CHF 20,000.

**"Revenue-Share Period"** means, in respect of each Attributed Customer, the period of **seven (7) years** starting on the date PWI issues the first paid invoice under the first Qualifying Contract with that Customer.

## 2. The Advisor's Role

**2.1** The Advisor's role under this Agreement combines two functions:

(a) **Introducer**: identifying and introducing PWI to Prospective Customers, using the Advisor's own network and professional standing, in a manner that is timely, warm and materially causative in initiating commercial discussions.

(b) **Advisor**: providing ongoing strategic advisory support with respect to Attributed Customers throughout the commercial cycle, including but not limited to: attending or supporting sales meetings on reasonable request, sharing informed commentary on customer dynamics, and helping PWI navigate the customer relationship.

**2.2** The Advisor commits to a reasonable and good-faith effort. The Parties acknowledge that enterprise sales cycles typically span 6–18 months and that no minimum activity, revenue outcome or number of introductions is guaranteed by the Advisor.

**2.3** The Advisor does **not** hold authority to bind PWI, negotiate commercial terms on PWI's behalf, sign contracts, make pricing commitments, or make legally binding representations to any Prospective Customer. Any such statements must be expressly authorised by PWI in writing on a case-by-case basis.

**2.4** The Advisor does **not** have any development, product-management, product-support, engineering, employment or fiduciary role with PWI.

## 3. Introductions and Attribution

**3.1** For an introduction to qualify as an Advisor Introduction, it must be:

(a) documented in writing (email or equivalent);
(b) made to a specifically named individual (not a general company address);
(c) accompanied by, or immediately followed by, a written notice to PWI identifying the Prospective Customer, the named individual, and the date of introduction;
(d) made at a time when the Prospective Customer meets the definition in section 1 (i.e., not already an active PWI account or opportunity).

**3.2** PWI will maintain a shared "**Introductions Register**" — a simple ledger listing each Advisor Introduction, its date, the Prospective Customer, the named contact, and the status of any follow-up. PWI will share the Introductions Register with the Advisor no less than quarterly.

**3.3** If PWI is already in active discussions with a Prospective Customer at the time of an Advisor Introduction, PWI will inform the Advisor within fourteen (14) days and the introduction shall not create an attribution claim. PWI will provide sufficient evidence (e.g. dated correspondence, CRM entries) to establish prior contact.

**3.4** After the end of the Attribution Window, if no Qualifying Contract has been signed with the Prospective Customer, that Advisor Introduction lapses and no revenue share arises. The Advisor may make a fresh Advisor Introduction to the same Customer at any later date, restarting a new Attribution Window.

## 4. Compensation — Tiered Revenue Share

**4.1** In consideration of the services under section 2, PWI shall pay the Advisor a revenue share on Attributable Revenue, calculated on a *cumulative-lifetime* basis across **all** Attributed Customers under the following tiered structure:

| Cumulative Attributable Revenue (lifetime, all customers) | Advisor's share |
|---|---|
| CHF 0 – 1,000,000 | **22 %** |
| CHF 1,000,001 – 3,000,000 | **17 %** |
| Above CHF 3,000,000 | **12 %** |

**4.2** For clarity: the tiers apply to cumulative Attributable Revenue across all Attributed Customers combined, not per customer. Each incremental CHF of Attributable Revenue is compensated at the tier applicable to the cumulative total at that time.

**4.3** The revenue share applies to renewals, expansions, upsells, add-on modules, additional users, additional entities and other extensions of the initial Qualifying Contract with an Attributed Customer, provided the corresponding Net Revenue is collected within that Customer's Revenue-Share Period.

**4.4** The revenue share is calculated only on Net Revenue actually collected by PWI. Uncollected receivables, bad debts, refunded amounts and chargebacks do not trigger a revenue share; where a revenue share has already been paid on amounts subsequently refunded or written off, the corresponding amount is credited against future amounts payable to the Advisor or, if none, invoiced back.

## 5. Compensation — Milestone Bonuses

In addition to the revenue share under section 4, PWI shall pay the Advisor the following one-time milestone bonuses:

| Milestone | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Signature of the first Qualifying Contract with any Attributed Customer | **CHF 15,000** |
| Cumulative Attributable Revenue reaches CHF 250,000 | **CHF 35,000** |
| Cumulative Attributable Revenue reaches CHF 1,000,000 | **CHF 75,000** |

Each milestone bonus is payable **once** and is payable within thirty (30) days of the milestone being met.

## 6. Payment Terms

**6.1** The revenue share is calculated and paid **quarterly in arrears**. Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, PWI will send the Advisor a written statement setting out, for that quarter:

(a) Attributable Revenue collected during the quarter, itemised by Attributed Customer;
(b) the running cumulative Attributable Revenue and the applicable tier(s);
(c) the amount of revenue share payable for the quarter;
(d) any adjustments (e.g. refunds, chargebacks, corrections);
(e) any milestone bonus triggered during the quarter.

**6.2** The Advisor shall invoice PWI for the amounts shown in the statement within a reasonable time, and PWI shall pay the invoice within **thirty (30) days** of receipt, subject to the invoice complying with applicable formal and VAT requirements.

**6.3** All amounts are in **Swiss Francs (CHF)**. Amounts collected by PWI in other currencies are converted to CHF at PWI's applicable accounting exchange rate for the relevant period, using the mid-market rate as of the last business day of the quarter.

**6.4** All amounts are stated **exclusive** of any VAT and other taxes that may be owed by the Advisor. Where the Advisor is subject to VAT, the invoice shall separately show VAT.

**6.5** Payment shall be made by SEPA / SIC bank transfer to a bank account nominated by the Advisor in writing.

**6.6** In case of late payment by PWI, default interest of 5 % p.a. under Art. 104 CO applies, without further notice being required.

## 7. Preferred-Channel Status

**7.1** During the term of this Agreement, and provided that the Advisor is actively engaged (defined as at least one Advisor Introduction of a bona-fide Prospective Customer every six (6) months), PWI shall not:

(a) engage another external introducer, finder, agent or channel partner on materially similar economic terms with respect to enterprise customers in the banking-software and wealth-management sector, without first offering the Advisor the opportunity to source the same customer within a reasonable period;

(b) hire a dedicated internal enterprise business-development or channel-partnerships lead without giving the Advisor at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

**7.2** For clarity, PWI remains fully free to (i) sell the Software directly to any customer sourced by PWI's own efforts, (ii) run inbound marketing and advertising, (iii) sell via any online store, marketplace or distribution channel, and (iv) engage engineering, product, marketing, or support staff of any nature.

## 8. Non-Solicitation of Competing Software

During the term of this Agreement, the Advisor shall not knowingly introduce, promote or actively facilitate the sale of any commercially available financial-planning software product that is *directly and materially competitive* with the Software to any Attributed Customer or Prospective Customer.

For clarity, this section 8 does **not** prevent the Advisor from (i) accepting other consulting or advisory roles, including in the broader financial-services sector, (ii) working with core-banking, CRM, portfolio-management or other adjacent software vendors that are not directly and materially competitive with the Software, or (iii) informing customers about alternatives if directly asked and it would be a breach of professional duty to remain silent.

## 9. Term

**9.1** This Agreement takes effect on [effective date] (the "**Effective Date**").

**9.2** The Agreement is entered into for an **initial term of three (3) years** from the Effective Date, automatically renewing for successive one-year terms, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

**9.3** Either Party may terminate the Agreement:

(a) at any time by giving the other Party ninety (90) days' prior written notice;
(b) with immediate effect for cause, in the event of a material breach by the other Party that is not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice, or in the event of insolvency, bankruptcy or comparable proceedings of the other Party.

## 10. Consequences of Termination

**10.1** Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, **the Advisor's right to receive revenue share and any earned but unpaid milestone bonuses on Attributed Customers already in place at the date of termination survives** and continues throughout the applicable Revenue-Share Period of each such Customer, subject to the terms of sections 4–6.

**10.2** No new Advisor Introductions may be made after termination, and no attribution may arise from any introduction after the termination date.

**10.3** Termination for cause by PWI following a material and uncured breach by the Advisor (in particular, a breach of the anti-corruption or confidentiality obligations) entitles PWI, in addition to any other remedy, to suspend or offset future revenue-share payments to the extent commensurate with the loss caused.

**10.4** Sections 8 (Non-Solicitation), 11 (Anti-Corruption), 13 (Confidentiality), 15 (Data Protection), 16 (Independent Contractor / Tax) and 18 (Governing Law and Jurisdiction) survive termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 11. Anti-Corruption and Ethical Conduct

**11.1** The Advisor confirms that:

(a) all consideration payable under this Agreement is due exclusively to the Advisor in his personal capacity;
(b) no portion of any payment under this Agreement has been, is being, or will be onward-paid, shared, gifted, or otherwise made available, directly or indirectly, to any employee, director, agent, officer, family member, or connected person of any Prospective Customer or Attributed Customer, including but not limited to the "senior Avaloq employee" and any equivalent contact at any other Prospective Customer;
(c) no benefit, favour, gift, entertainment or other advantage has been or will be provided by the Advisor to any such person in connection with facilitating an introduction, decision or transaction;
(d) the Advisor will disclose to PWI any actual or apparent conflict of interest that could reasonably affect the Advisor's introductions or advice, promptly upon becoming aware of it.

**11.2** PWI reserves the right, in serious commercial discussions with a Prospective Customer, to disclose the existence and structure of this Agreement to that Customer's compliance function or equivalent, where PWI reasonably considers it appropriate. The Advisor consents to such disclosure and agrees to cooperate.

**11.3** A breach of section 11.1 (a) to (d) constitutes a material breach permitting immediate termination for cause under section 9.3 (b), and entitles PWI to recover any payments made in respect of transactions tainted by the breach.

**11.4** The Parties will comply with all applicable Swiss and international anti-bribery, anti-corruption and anti-money-laundering laws, including but not limited to the Swiss Criminal Code (in particular Arts. 322septies and 322octies), the Swiss Anti-Money-Laundering Act (AMLA), the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the U.K. Bribery Act 2010, in each case to the extent applicable.

## 12. Independent Contractor Status and Tax

**12.1** The Advisor is engaged as an **independent contractor** and is not, and shall not be treated as, an employee, worker, agent (in a legally binding sense), officer or partner of PWI.

**12.2** The Advisor is solely responsible for:

(a) registering with the relevant Swiss social-security authorities as *Selbständigerwerbender* (self-employed), or invoicing PWI through his own commercial entity;
(b) paying all applicable income tax, social-security contributions (AHV, IV, EO, BVG, KTG etc.), and VAT (MWST) attributable to payments under this Agreement;
(c) maintaining his own insurance cover as required by law and prudence, including professional liability insurance where relevant.

**12.3** The Advisor confirms his self-employment/business status by providing PWI, before the first invoice is paid, with a copy of his valid registration as *Selbständigerwerbender* with the competent SVA (or comparable non-Swiss registration), or evidence of the commercial entity through which he will invoice.

**12.4** If the Advisor's tax or social-security status is subsequently reclassified by any competent authority such that PWI becomes liable for social-security contributions on payments already made, the Advisor shall indemnify PWI for any such contributions, plus interest and penalties, save where the reclassification results from PWI's own contractual arrangement or instructions.

**12.5** The Advisor is free to render services to third parties in parallel, subject to the limitations set out in section 8.

## 13. Confidentiality

**13.1** Each Party shall treat as strictly confidential any non-public information of the other Party, including in particular: business plans, product roadmaps, pricing, customer identities and commercial terms, financial information, and the terms of this Agreement.

**13.2** The confidentiality obligation continues for **five (5) years** after termination or expiry of this Agreement.

**13.3** Excluded from the confidentiality obligation is information that (i) is already publicly known through no breach of this Agreement, (ii) was already known to the receiving Party without a duty of confidence, (iii) has been independently developed, or (iv) must be disclosed to comply with mandatory law, court order or a regulatory obligation (in which case the disclosing Party will, where lawful, notify the other Party in advance).

**13.4** The Advisor may disclose the *existence* of this Agreement and his role in general terms (for example on his professional CV or public profile), but shall not disclose commercial terms, revenue figures, customer identities or the identity of Attributed Customers.

## 14. Intellectual Property

**14.1** All intellectual property in and to the Software, PWI's brand, its marketing materials, its documentation and its client-facing outputs remains the exclusive property of PWI.

**14.2** The Advisor is granted no licence or right to the Software beyond a personal, non-transferable right to use and demonstrate the Software as reasonably required to perform his services under this Agreement.

**14.3** Any strategic input, feedback, ideas or suggestions provided by the Advisor to PWI shall be non-confidential and freely usable by PWI without compensation beyond that provided under this Agreement, unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing at the time of provision.

## 15. Data Protection

Each Party shall comply with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP/nDSG) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In particular, where the Advisor obtains any personal data of PWI representatives or Prospective Customers in the course of his work, he shall process it strictly for the purposes of this Agreement, apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, and not share it with any third party without lawful basis.

## 16. Records and Audit

**16.1** PWI shall maintain accurate books and records of all Attributable Revenue for the duration of the Revenue-Share Period for each Attributed Customer, plus **three (3) years** thereafter.

**16.2** Once per calendar year, on at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, the Advisor may appoint an independent Swiss-qualified auditor (bound by professional confidentiality) to review the records relevant to the calculation of amounts payable under this Agreement.

**16.3** The audit is at the Advisor's cost, save where the audit reveals an underpayment of more than **5 %** of the amount actually due for the period audited, in which case PWI shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit and pay the shortfall plus default interest at 5 % p.a.

## 17. General Provisions

**17.1** **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement (together with any exhibits) contains the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and replaces any prior understandings, whether oral or written.

**17.2** **Amendments.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Email exchanges do **not** suffice unless expressly agreed in writing.

**17.3** **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Notwithstanding the foregoing, PWI may assign this Agreement in connection with a sale of all or substantially all of its business or assets, provided the assignee assumes all obligations toward the Advisor in writing.

**17.4** **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by email (with confirmation of receipt) or by registered post to the addresses shown in the preamble, or such other address as either Party may notify to the other from time to time.

**17.5** **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that performance is prevented by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that the affected Party gives prompt notice.

**17.6** **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect and the Parties shall replace the invalid provision with a valid one that reflects the original commercial intent as closely as possible.

**17.7** **No Waiver.** Failure or delay by either Party to enforce any right does not constitute a waiver of that right.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**18.1** This Agreement is governed by, and shall be construed in accordance with, the substantive laws of **Switzerland**, to the exclusion of its rules of conflict of laws.

**18.2** The exclusive place of jurisdiction for any dispute arising from or in connection with this Agreement is **Zurich, Switzerland**.

**18.3** The Parties agree to first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct negotiation before initiating court proceedings.
